# ADR 0003: reduced-coordinate Carnot mechanism

Status: accepted, 2026-09-28.

A periodic quintic cam supplies the four ideal area waypoints and a
right-continuous branch selector. The displayed piston wall and its thermal
policy read the same shaft coordinate.

Controlled mode treats wall work as external motor work. Free mode includes
shaft inertia in the generalized collision effective mass and applies piston
impulses through the cam derivative. Load work leaves the free apparatus and is
entered with the opposite sign in the first-law ledger.
